Privacy Notice

How we handle your information.

People who contact us may share information that is personal, sensitive and sometimes dangerous to disclose. This notice explains what we collect, why, who we share it with and the rights you have.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Global Accountability Pathways CIC (“GAP”, “we”, “us”), a community interest company registered in England and Wales, company number 17596495. Our registered office is 14 New Wharf Road, London N1 9RT.

We are the controller of the personal information described in this notice. This means we decide how and why it is used, and we are responsible for protecting it under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. What information we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us, or what we receive about a matter you have raised with us.

When you contact us

  • Your name, email address and any other contact details you provide.
  • The content of your messages and any documents, images or other files you send.
  • Information about your organization, if you contact us on its behalf.

When you bring a lead or request

Information shared with us about possible crimes or human rights violations may include special category data (such as information about health, ethnic origin, religious or political beliefs) and information about alleged criminal conduct. It may concern you, or other people such as witnesses, victims or alleged perpetrators.

When you fund or partner with us

Contact details and correspondence with you or your organization, and any information needed to manage funding or a partnership.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and keep in touch about it.
  • To assess whether a lead or request is something we can take forward, and what further work it would need.
  • To investigate selected matters and develop legal and advocacy strategies, including preserving evidence for future proceedings.
  • With your agreement, to refer a matter to, or work on it with, partners or authorities.
  • To manage relationships with funders and partners.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for marketing without your consent.

4. Our legal bases

Data protection law requires us to have a lawful basis for each use of personal information. Depending on the situation, we rely on:

  • Consent – where you have agreed to a specific use. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests – to respond to enquiries, assess leads and run our organization, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation – where we must process information to comply with the law.

For special category data, we rely on your explicit consent or, where necessary, the condition for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. Where we handle information about alleged criminal offences, we do so only where a condition in Schedule 1 of the Data Protection Act 2018 applies.

5. Who we share information with

We treat what you share with us as confidential. We will not pass information about you or your matter to anyone outside GAP without your informed consent, except where the law requires it or where it is needed to protect someone from serious harm.

With your consent, we may share information with:

  • Lawyers, investigators, researchers and other specialists working with us on a matter.
  • Partner organizations better placed to take a matter forward.
  • Prosecutors, courts or other authorities with jurisdiction over a matter.

We also use service providers who host our website and email. They process information only on our instructions and must keep it secure.

6. International transfers

Our work is transnational, so information may need to be sent to people or authorities outside the UK. When this happens, we use the safeguards required by UK data protection law, such as transfers to countries the UK recognises as providing adequate protection or approved contractual terms. In some cases a transfer may be necessary for legal claims or made with your explicit consent after we have explained the risks.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for. General enquiries that do not lead to further work are deleted once they are resolved and no longer needed.

Evidence connected to an investigation may need to be kept for longer, because proceedings for serious international crimes can begin many years after the events. Where this applies, we review what we hold regularly and will discuss retention with you.

8. Keeping information secure

We use technical and organizational measures to protect information against loss, misuse and unauthorized access, and we limit access to people who need it for their work.

Before you send sensitive information. Ordinary email is not fully secure. If your information could put you or others at risk, please first send a short message without details, and we will arrange a safer way to share it.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete your information.
  • Ask us to restrict how we use it.
  • Object to our use of it where we rely on legitimate interests.
  • Receive information you gave us in a portable format, in some circumstances.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

Some rights are limited in certain situations, for example where information is subject to legal professional privilege or where disclosure would affect another person’s rights or safety. If we cannot meet a request in full, we will explain why. We aim to respond within one month.

10. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking tools, and it has no forms that collect information.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers and shares your IP address with Google. You can read how Google handles this in the Google Privacy Policy. Our hosting provider may also keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for security purposes.

11. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as our work develops. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.

12. Contact and complaints

For questions about this notice or to exercise your rights, contact us at hello@gapjustice.org or write to Global Accountability Pathways CIC, 14 New Wharf Road, London N1 9RT.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
